# Colony Privacy Policy

**Effective 29 September 2026**

Colony is published by Yoav Peretz, an individual developer ("I", "me"). This policy covers the
Colony app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro.

## The short version

**Colony collects no data about you.** Your projects, tasks, messages and contacts live on your
devices and in your own private iCloud database. I have no server, and I cannot read, sell or share
your data. Colony has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What Colony stores, and where

| What | Where it's stored | Who can read it |
|---|---|---|
| Projects, lists, tasks, channels, messages, contacts you add, activity history | On your device, synced through your **private iCloud database** (CloudKit) | You, on devices signed in to your Apple Account |
| Preferences: name, workspace name, appearance, fonts, sidebar layout | On your device and in **iCloud key-value storage** | You, on devices signed in to your Apple Account |

iCloud is operated by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
Records in a private iCloud database are only available to the Apple Account that owns them.
If you aren't signed in to iCloud, Colony keeps everything on the device only.

## Permissions Colony asks for

Colony asks for access only when you use the feature that needs it, and you can turn access off at
any time in Settings (iOS, iPadOS, visionOS) or System Settings › Privacy & Security (macOS).

- **Contacts.** Only used when you choose **Import from Contacts**. Colony reads names,
  company, job title, the first email address, the first phone number and the contact photo,
  so you can pick people to import. Only the people you select are copied into Colony. Colony never
  changes your address book.
- **Reminders.** Only used if you turn on **Mirror tasks to Reminders**. Colony writes a task's
  title, notes, project name, priority and due date to your default Reminders list, and updates or
  removes that reminder when the task changes. Apple syncs your Reminders under its own policy.
- **Music.** The player at the bottom of the sidebar shows what's playing on your device (title,
  artist, artwork, position) and sends play, pause, skip, seek and mute to it. On the Mac it talks to
  the Music app (macOS asks you first); on iPhone and iPad it uses the system music player. This
  information is only held in memory while Colony is open. It is never stored, synced or sent anywhere.
- **Notifications.** Only used if you turn on **Notify me when tasks are due**. Colony schedules the
  alerts on your device with Apple's notification system; the task's title, project and notes are
  shown in the alert. Nothing is sent to a server.
- **Calendar.** Only used if you turn on **Show tasks in Calendar** or **Show automation schedules**.
  Colony creates its own "Colony" calendar in your calendar account and adds dated tasks (title,
  project, notes, due date) and automation schedules to it. It reads that calendar to keep it up to
  date and never changes your other calendars. Turning both off deletes the Colony calendar. Apple
  syncs your calendars under its own policy.

When you tap to email, call or FaceTime a contact, Colony hands the address or number to Apple's
Mail, Phone or FaceTime app. Colony does not see or record what happens next.

Colony also reads the list of fonts installed on your device, so you can pick one in
Settings › Appearance. That list never leaves the device.

## What Colony does not do

- No analytics, telemetry or usage tracking.
- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
- No third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No accounts other than your Apple Account. Colony never sees your Apple Account credentials.
- No selling or sharing of personal data, because I never receive any.

**Crash reports.** If you have turned on *Share with App Developers* in your device's Analytics
settings, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports and diagnostics. That setting is controlled by
you and governed by Apple's policy. The reports contain no content from Colony.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it.

- **Delete individual items** inside Colony: projects, tasks, messages and contacts. Deletions sync to
  your other devices.
- **Delete everything Colony stored in iCloud:**
  - **iPhone or iPad:** Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Colony › Delete Data from iCloud.
  - **Mac:** System Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage › Colony › Delete.
- **Delete everything on a device:** delete the app. This removes the local copy.

Because I don't hold your data, I can't access, export or delete it for you. The steps above give
you full control.

## Children

Colony is a productivity app for general audiences. It is not directed at children and collects
no data from anyone, including children.
