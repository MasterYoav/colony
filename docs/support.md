# Colony Support

## Contact

Email **[realyoavperetz@gmail.com](mailto:realyoavperetz@gmail.com)**, or
[open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/MasterYoav/colony/issues). I usually reply within a few days.

Please include your device, the operating system version, and the app version shown at the bottom of
Settings in Colony.

## Common questions

**My data isn't showing up on another device.**
Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and that
Colony is allowed to use iCloud:
- **iPhone or iPad:** Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Saved to iCloud.
- **Mac:** System Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

In Colony, Settings › iCloud shows the current sync status, and **Refresh** checks again.

**Contacts import or Reminders says access is denied.**
Colony can only ask once. Turn access on yourself:
- **iPhone or iPad:** Settings › Privacy & Security › Contacts (or Reminders) › Colony.
- **Mac:** System Settings › Privacy & Security › Contacts (or Reminders).

**How do I delete all my data?**
See [Keeping and deleting your data](../privacy/#keeping-and-deleting-your-data) in the privacy policy.
